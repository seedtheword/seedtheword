    About Our Ministry

    Sharing faith, one Bible at a time.

    Who We Are

    We are a community-driven Bible gifting ministry dedicated to placing the Word of God in the hands of those who are new to faith. We believe that every newcomer deserves a warm, personalized welcome — and that a Bible, thoughtfully prepared, can be that gift.

    Our mission is to remove financial barriers so that no one is denied access to Scripture. Through community donations and volunteer hearts, we fund, customize, and deliver Bible bundles to newcomers across our community and beyond.

    We believe in the transformative power of the Word, the generosity of believers, and the importance of walking alongside those who are just beginning their faith journey.

    How We Serve

    📖

    Bible Gifting

    We curate and customize Bible bundles as welcoming gifts for newcomers to faith — removing financial barriers so everyone can receive the Word.

    👥

    Community

    We nurture an online community of believers for daily encouragement, Bible reading, and mutual support on the journey of faith.

    🙏

    Prayer Support

    We pray with and for those who reach out — whether they are new to faith, seeking answers, or simply in need of encouragement.
