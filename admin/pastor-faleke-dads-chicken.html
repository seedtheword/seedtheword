Our Story

Where Africa Meets Your Table

Born from a father's passion, brought to life by the whole family. Co-owned and operated — every plate we serve carries the spice, the tradition, and the love of West African home cooking.

👨‍🍳

Family Recipe

Generations of West African cooking wisdom — passed down, refined, and made fresh every day. The kind of food that makes you feel at home even if you've never been to Nigeria.

🌍

Welcoming to Everyone

No labels, no barriers. Open to all people, all backgrounds, all appetites. We believe good food brings people together — and African cuisine does it better than most.

What We Serve

Signature Menu

🌶️

Dad's Signature Spicy Chicken Signature

The dish that started it all. Slow-marinated in bold West African spice, grilled over open flame. The flavor that keeps people coming back.

🍚

Jollof Rice & Stew Fan Fav

Smoky, rich, tomato-based rice — the national pride of West Africa. Served with your choice of protein.

🥘

Nigerian Pepper Soup

Rich, aromatic broth with goat or chicken. Warming, healing, deeply traditional. The bowl that reminds you what food is for.

🍲

Egusi Soup

Ground melon seeds, fresh greens, and seasoned proteins in rich palm oil. A cornerstone of Nigerian home cooking.

🫓

Puff Puff & Chin Chin Dessert

Sweet fried dough bites — the West African classic for snacking and celebrating. Light, golden, impossible to stop at one.
