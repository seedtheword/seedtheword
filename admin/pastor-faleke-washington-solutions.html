Our Structure

For-Profit Gains,
Nonprofit Purpose

A clear two-entity structure that lets us do more for the communities we serve.

The Faleke's & Washington Solutions LLC is a separate for-profit entity from W.A.V.E. nonprofit. This structure allows the LLC to engage in commercial contracts, merchandise, and earned income that the nonprofit legally cannot.

A portion of the LLC's earnings supports W.A.V.E — West African Vision & Empowerment — funding farm training, pastoral equipping, and displaced Christian relief in Nigeria. For-profit work enabling nonprofit impact.
