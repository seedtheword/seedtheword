Our Structure

For-Profit Gains, Nonprofit Purpose

The Faleke's & Washington Solutions LLC is a separate for-profit entity from W.A.V.E. nonprofit. This structure allows the LLC to engage in commercial contracts, merchandise, and earned income activities that the nonprofit cannot.

A portion of the LLC's earnings is directed toward sponsoring W.A.V.E — West African Vision & Empowerment — supporting displaced Christians in Nigeria through farm training, pastoral equipping, and youth empowerment programs.

⚖️

Compliance note: For-profit merchandise and commercial services are offered through this LLC only — not through the W.A.V.E. nonprofit. Donors to W.A.V.E. support charitable mission. Commercial clients engage the LLC. Both work together toward the same vision.
