What We Do

🏗️

Government Contracting

Contract sourcing from state and federal government agencies. We pursue service contracts that align with our expertise in housing, community development, and operational services.

🏠

Section 8 Refurbishment

Refurbishing Section 8 housing units to livable, dignified standards. Every family deserves a safe place to call home — we make that happen through quality restoration work.

🌍

IDP Support Solutions

Services addressing the needs of internally displaced people (IDP) — connecting resources, housing, and operational support to those who have been forced from their homes.

📋

Administrative & Operations

Professional operational services supporting community organizations, nonprofits, and government-adjacent entities seeking reliable, mission-aligned partners.

For-profit gains, nonprofit purpose. The Faleke's & Washington Solutions LLC operates as a separate for-profit entity. A portion of earnings is directed toward sponsoring W.A.V.E — West African Vision & Empowerment — supporting displaced Christians in Nigeria.

This structure allows the LLC to engage in commercial contracts, merchandise, and earned income activities that the nonprofit cannot — while ensuring those gains extend the mission of empowerment, relief, and Gospel work.

Internally Displaced People (IDP) Focus

Whether displaced by conflict in West Africa or facing housing instability in Washington State, our work is oriented toward people who have lost their footing. We believe stable housing and community infrastructure are foundational — to healing, to family, and to faith.

Partner With Us

We are actively seeking government contracts, community partnerships, and Section 8 refurbishment projects. If you are a property owner, government agency, or community organization — let's talk.
