Nonprofit · Nigeria · West Africa

W.A.V.E

West African Vision & Empowerment

Raising support for displaced and persecuted Christians in West Africa. Empowering youth. Training leaders. Preaching truth.

⚠️ The Crisis We're Responding To

Islamic militant groups are waging genocide against Nigerian Christians — kidnapping families, burning villages, displacing communities, and forcing conversion on teenagers and children. W.A.V.E. exists to respond to this crisis with tangible support, training, and the Gospel.

Our Mission Pillars

🛡️

Protect the Persecuted

Raise awareness and support for displaced Nigerian Christians who have lost homes, families, and livelihoods to militant violence.

🌱

Youth Empowerment

50–60% youth unemployment in affected regions. W.A.V.E. trains young people to be self-sufficient through vocational and agricultural programs.

📖

Sound Doctrine

Training pastors and ministers in Biblical truth — pushing back against prosperity gospel that weaponizes Christianity and misleads believers in poverty.

🎓

Student Access

Through Fellowship of Christian Students and campus alumni networks — reaching elementary through university students with servant-leadership training.

Programs

🌾 Farm Training Program

A 3-month crash course on sustainable farming — displaced youth learn agricultural skills, feed themselves, and build toward economic independence. The farm feeds the students while they train.

⛪ Pastoral Training

Equipping pastors and church leaders with sound Biblical teaching within the Pentecostal tradition. Fighting false doctrine with truth and discipleship.

🏫 Student Leadership

Training students in elementary, secondary, and university settings to lead as servants — rooted in the image of Christ, not cultural prosperity narratives.

🤝 Organizational Access

Through C.A.N. (Christian Association of Nigeria) and P.F.N. (Pentecostal Fellowship of Nigeria) — institutional access to reach and train across the entire Nigerian church body.

About Our Founder

A former Vice President of the student union and alumnus of the Fellowship of Christian Students — with 10 years of pastoral ministry in Nigeria. The founder built W.A.V.E. from lived experience in the crisis, with established relationships inside C.A.N. and P.F.N. that open doors for training and ministry across West Africa.

Stand With Nigerian Christians

Your support funds farm land, training programs, displaced family relief, and the equipping of faithful pastors who refuse to compromise the Gospel.
