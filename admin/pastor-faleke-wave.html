The Crisis We Are Responding To

Islamic militant groups are waging genocide against Nigerian Christians — kidnapping families, burning villages, displacing entire communities, and forcing children and teenagers into conversion. W.A.V.E. responds to this crisis with tangible support, Gospel-centered training, and unwavering truth.

How We Work

Programs & Initiatives

Four areas where W.A.V.E. channels its work — all rooted in sound Biblical doctrine and practical compassion for suffering communities.

🌿

Farm Training Program

A 3-month crash course in sustainable farming. Displaced youth learn agricultural skills, feed themselves during training, and build toward economic independence — trusting God to provide while equipping His people to work.

⛪️

Pastoral Training

Equipping pastors and ministers with sound Biblical doctrine — pushing back against prosperity gospel that weaponizes Christianity and misleads believers living in poverty. We teach what Scripture actually says.

🏫

Student Leadership

Through Fellowship of Christian Students alumni networks — training elementary through university students to lead as servants, rooted in the image of Christ, not cultural success narratives.

🤝

C.A.N. & P.F.N. Access

Through the Christian Association of Nigeria and Pentecostal Fellowship of Nigeria — institutional access to reach and train across the entire Nigerian church body. Every Christian in Nigeria belongs to these organizations.

Built From the Inside

A former Vice President of the student union and alumnus of the Fellowship of Christian Students — with 10 years of pastoral ministry in Nigeria. Pastor Faleke built W.A.V.E. from lived experience in the crisis, with established relationships inside C.A.N. and P.F.N. that open doors for training and ministry across West Africa.

Currently based in Washington State, partnering with local ministries including Seed the Word to build a bridge between West African persecuted communities and American churches that want to stand with their brothers and sisters in Christ.

The work is not theoretical. The crisis is real. The response is organized, trained, and already underway.
