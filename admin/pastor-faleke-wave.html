The Crisis We Are Responding To

Islamic militant groups are waging genocide against Nigerian Christians — kidnapping families, burning villages, displacing communities, and forcing children into conversion. W.A.V.E. responds with tangible support, Gospel-centered training, and the truth that sets people free.

What We Stand For

Our Mission Pillars

Four areas where W.A.V.E. directs its work — all rooted in Biblical truth and practical compassion for those who have suffered most.

🌿

Youth Empowerment & Training

50–60% youth unemployment in affected regions. W.A.V.E. trains young people to be self-sufficient through vocational and agricultural programs — building futures from the ground up.

📖

Sound Biblical Doctrine

Pushing back against prosperity gospel that weaponizes Christianity and misleads believers living in poverty. We teach what Scripture actually says — because truth matters most when life is hardest.

⛪️

Pastoral & Minister Training

Equipping pastors and church leaders within the Pentecostal tradition with sound theology and practical ministry tools — building the capacity of the church to serve its own communities.

🏫

Student Leadership Development

Through Fellowship of Christian Students alumni networks — reaching elementary through university students with servant-leadership training rooted in the image of Christ, not prosperity narratives.

How We Work

Programs & Initiatives

Practical programs that address both spiritual and physical hunger — because W.A.V.E. was built for the whole person, not just the soul.

🌿 3-Month Farm Training Program

Displaced youth learn sustainable farming skills, feed themselves during training, and build toward economic independence. The farm provides nourishment and a career pathway — trusting God to provide while equipping His people to work with dignity.

⛪️ Pastoral Training Institutes

Equipping pastors and ministers with sound Biblical doctrine within the Pentecostal movement — combating false teaching with truth, and building a generation of leaders who serve rather than exploit their congregations.

🌏 C.A.N. & P.F.N. Church Access

Through the Christian Association of Nigeria and Pentecostal Fellowship of Nigeria — institutional access to reach and train across the entire Nigerian church body. All Christians in Nigeria belong to these two bodies, giving W.A.V.E. unmatched reach.

🏫 Campus Student Training

Through Fellowship of Christian Students alumni networks — W.A.V.E. trains students from elementary through university to lead as servants of Christ. The goal is to develop leaders who bear the image of Christ, not cultural success metrics.

Built From the Inside

A former Vice President of the student union and alumnus of the Fellowship of Christian Students — with 10 years of pastoral ministry in Nigeria. Pastor Faleke built W.A.V.E. from lived experience in the crisis, with established relationships inside C.A.N. and P.F.N. that open doors for training and ministry across West Africa.

Currently based in Washington State, partnering with local ministries including Seed the Word Ministry to build a bridge between West African persecuted communities and American churches that want to stand with their brothers and sisters in Christ.

The work is not theoretical. The crisis is real. The response is organized, trained, and already underway.
