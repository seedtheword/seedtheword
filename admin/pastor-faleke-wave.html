⚠️ The Crisis We Are Responding To

Islamic militant groups are waging genocide against Nigerian Christians — kidnapping families, burning villages, displacing entire communities, and forcing children and teenagers into conversion. W.A.V.E. exists to respond to this crisis with tangible support, Gospel-centered training, and the truth.

What We Stand For

Our Mission Pillars

Four areas where W.A.V.E. focuses its work — all rooted in sound Biblical doctrine and practical compassion.

🛡️

Protect the Persecuted

Raise awareness and mobilize support for displaced Nigerian Christians who have lost homes, families, and livelihoods to militant violence.

🌱

Youth Empowerment

50–60% youth unemployment in affected regions. W.A.V.E. trains young people to be self-sufficient through vocational and agricultural programs.

📖

Sound Doctrine

Pushing back against prosperity gospel that weaponizes Christianity and misleads believers living in poverty. We teach what Scripture actually says.

🎓

Student Leadership

Through Fellowship of Christian Students alumni networks — reaching elementary through university students with servant-leadership training rooted in the image of Christ.

How We Work

Programs & Initiatives

🌾 3-Month Farm Training Program

Displaced youth learn sustainable farming skills, feed themselves during training, and build toward economic independence. The farm provides both nourishment and a career pathway — trusting God to provide, while teaching people to work the land.

⛪ Pastoral & Minister Training

Equipping pastors and church leaders with sound Biblical teaching within the Pentecostal tradition — fighting false prosperity doctrine with truth, discipleship, and practical ministry tools.

🏫 Campus Student Leadership

Training students in elementary, secondary, and university settings to lead as servants — built on the image of Christ, not cultural success narratives. Alumni of Fellowship of Christian Students open the doors.

🤝 C.A.N. & P.F.N. Access

Through the Christian Association of Nigeria and Pentecostal Fellowship of Nigeria — institutional access to reach and train across the entire Nigerian church body. These organizations represent all Christians in Nigeria.

About the Founder

A former Vice President of the student union and alumnus of the Fellowship of Christian Students — with 10 years of pastoral ministry in Nigeria. Built W.A.V.E. from lived experience in the crisis, with established relationships inside C.A.N. and P.F.N. that open doors for training and ministry across West Africa.

Currently based in Washington State, partnering with local ministries including Seed the Word to build a bridge between West African communities and American churches standing with persecuted believers.
