🚀 How to Get Plugged In

New here? Here's everything you need to join the community and stay connected.

💬

Join Our Telegram

Daily Bible discussion, prayer, and community chat — our most active space.

Step-by-step
  1. Download the Telegram app from your phone's app store (it's free).
  2. Sign up with your phone number and pick a display name.
  3. Tap the button below or search for @seedtheword in Telegram.
  4. Tap Join — you're in. Say hi to introduce yourself.
Join Telegram
📸

Follow on Instagram

Photos, videos, and behind-the-scenes from our outreach and events.

Step-by-step
  1. Download the Instagram app or visit instagram.com in your browser.
  2. Create a free account if you don't already have one.
  3. Tap the button below or search for @seedtheword.
  4. Tap Follow and turn on notifications to catch new posts.
Follow Instagram
📖

Read Along With Us

One chapter a day, Monday through Friday. Simple and sustainable.

Step-by-step
  1. Check this page (or our Telegram) each morning for today's chapter.
  2. Open your Bible — or use a free app like YouVersion or Bible.com.
  3. Read slowly. Look up one word or idea you don't understand.
  4. Share a verse that stood out in our Telegram group — we'd love to hear it.

📺

Watch the Livestream

Saturday afternoons on Twitch — Bible study, fellowship, and prayer together.

Step-by-step
  1. Visit twitch.tv/seedtheword — no account needed to watch.
  2. Create a free Twitch account if you want to chat with us live.
  3. Click Follow on our channel to get notified when we go live.
  4. Join us Saturdays at 2:00 PM Pacific Time.
