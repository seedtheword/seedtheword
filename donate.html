If you donate What happens next ↓
1

You tell us

How many Bibles, and whether you'd like to drop them off or have us pick them up. Two minutes, max.

2

We reach out

Within 48 hours, our team coordinates the handoff at a meeting, cookout, or a place that works for you.

3

They get read

Each Bible is paired with someone who asked for one — or distributed at our outreach events.

If you'd like to receive What happens next ↓
1

You share your story

A couple of honest sentences about why you'd like a Bible. We read every one.

2

Our team reviews

Within 48 hours, a team member reads your request prayerfully and gets back to you by email.

3

You receive yours

Drop-off in person, or mailed to your address — your choice on the next step. Always free.
