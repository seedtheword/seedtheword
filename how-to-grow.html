How to Grow

Water the seed. Daily reading, audio Bible chapters, weekly studies, and walking alongside fellow believers.

Daily Reading

Encounter the Word every day

Monday through Friday, we read one chapter of the New Testament together as a community. Saturday is Study Saturday — a deeper dive into the week's themes.

Our reading plan is simple: one chapter a day, in order, starting from Mark. No skipping, no rushing. Just showing up and letting God speak through His Word.

📖 Join the daily reading

Visit the Community page to see today's chapter, listen to the audio recording, and follow along with the group.

Audio Bible

Listen to the Word

We have audio recordings for every chapter — recorded by Gideons International — available for you to listen on your commute, at work, or before bed. No app needed, just press play.

Audio chapters are embedded directly into our Community page alongside each day's reading. For the full library, visit our Spotify podcast.

Study Saturday

Go deeper together

Every Saturday we gather (live on Twitch or in person) to study the week's reading using five lenses:

  • Historical — what was happening when this was written?
  • Linguistic — what do the original Greek/Hebrew words mean?
  • Interpretive — what is the author's intent?
  • Personal — what is God saying to me today?
  • External — how does this connect to the rest of Scripture?

🎙️ Watch on Twitch

Saturdays 7–10 PM Pacific. twitch.tv/seedtheword
