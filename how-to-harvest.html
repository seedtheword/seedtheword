Finding a home

New believers need more than we can offer — pastors, teaching, sacraments, a local body. We work across a variety of Bible-believing denominations and know how to help each person land well in a church that fits them. We connect them with:

  • A church home suited to their background and needs
  • Small groups & life groups for deeper community
  • Mentors for new believers
  • Prayer partners for accountability

Complex doctrine goes to trained pastors. We stay in love, prayer, and encouragement. Looking for a church? Reach out.
