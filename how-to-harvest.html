The Fruit

What does harvest look like?

Harvest is what happens when seeds grow. A conversation at a park becomes a prayer. A prayer becomes faith. Faith becomes a life surrendered to Christ. And that new believer needs a home — a church, a community, a place to be known and loved.

"The harvest truly is plenteous, but the labourers are few; pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he will send forth labourers into his harvest." — Matthew 9:37-38 KJV

We don't own the harvest — God does. Our job is to faithfully sow and water, then celebrate when He brings the increase.

Church Placement

Finding a home

As new believers grow, they need more than we can offer. They need ordained pastors, structured teaching, sacraments, and a local body. We help connect people with churches in their area:

  • Non-denominational Bible-believing churches
  • Small groups and life groups for deeper community
  • Mentorship relationships for new believers
  • Prayer partners for ongoing accountability

Complex doctrine goes to trained pastors. We stay in the lanes of love, prayer, and encouragement. If you're looking for a church, reach out — we'll help you find one.

Impact

By the numbers

Since March 2026, God has used our small team to:

  • Give away 600+ Bibles in 10+ languages
  • Partner with 4+ local ministries for outreach events

  • Train new sowers who are now giving away Bibles on their own

Every number is a person. To God be the glory.
