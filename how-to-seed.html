The Mission

What does it mean to Seed the Word?

Seeding is the first step — meeting people where they are and placing the Word of God into their hands. No pressure, no agenda beyond love. We share Bibles, pray with strangers, and trust God to grow what He starts.

Jesus said: "He that soweth the good seed is the Son of man; the field is the world." — Matthew 13:37-38 KJV

We're just sowers. The field is everywhere — parks, campuses, cookouts, bus stops, your workplace. Anywhere a person is willing to receive.

Evangelism Tools

Tools for the field

You don't need to be a theologian. You just need to care. Here are tools that make conversations easier:

  • GodTools App — simple conversation guides in 100+ languages. Download free →
  • Pocket Bibles — we provide New Testaments in English, Spanish, Hindi, Russian, Arabic, Farsi, and more.
  • Life Books — short gospel booklets perfect for someone who isn't ready for a full Bible.
  • Your testimony — nothing is more powerful than your own story of how Jesus met you.
