Seed the Word

Walking the Path of Light

"Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path."

— Psalm 119:105 KJV

This is the ministry of Jesus, mapped out. Plant the seed. Water it to grow. Gather the harvest into the local church body. Then do it again — for the next person He brings us.

  1. 🌱

    Phase 1

    Plant the Seed

    Evangelism & new believers

    We meet newcomers where they are — on the street, at work, in our homes — and share the Gospel simply and honestly. Grace first: we show Jesus through action before we speak theology. Bibles go out with every seed. Tuesday meetings are where this begins.

    Gift a Bible →
  2. 💧

    Phase 2

    Water to Grow

    Discipleship & deepening

    Once the seed is planted, we walk alongside newcomers through daily Bible reading, weekly fellowship, prayer, and worship. Saturday studies go deeper using our five-pillar framework: historical, linguistic, interpretive, personal, and external. Non-denominational (John 17:3), anchored in intimacy with Christ — becoming images of Him. As Paul reminds us: we plant and water, but God makes it grow.

    Study with us →
  3. 🌾

    Phase 3

    Gather the Harvest

    Placement & maturation

    As believers mature, we help place them into the body of Christ — a local church, a life group, a calling to serve. Complex doctrine goes to ordained leadership. We walk alongside, carrying each other through prayer and mentorship (1 Timothy 1:5). The harvest belongs to the Lord, and He calls laborers into it.

    Connect with a mentor →
  4. ↻

    Repeat

    For the next person He brings us.
