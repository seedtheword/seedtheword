Where to start reading

A 30-day walk through the parts of the Bible that meet a brand-new reader where they are. Day 1 is whatever day you pick this up. Read one short passage a day, in any order if a particular day calls.

"All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for teaching, rebuking, correcting and training in righteousness."

— 2 Timothy 3:16 NIV

The plan is grouped into four weeks. Each week has a quiet theme — who Jesus is, how God meets us, what salvation is, how to walk. There's no quiz at the end. If you miss a day, just pick up where you left off. The point is the meeting, not the streak.
