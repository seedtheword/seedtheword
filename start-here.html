20-Day / Month Reading Plan

Where to start reading

Four weeks. Five days each — Monday through Friday. Every Monday is a Bible Discussion Study (DBS) where we gather and dig in together. Tuesday through Friday is one focused passage with an anchor verse to carry with you.

"Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path."

— Psalm 119:105 ESV

The plan walks through four themes in order: who Jesus is, trusting God in prayer and praise, what salvation means, and then what the Christian walk looks like in daily life. There's no quiz at the end. If you miss a day, pick up where you left off. The point is the meeting, not the streak.

When you finish Day 20

Come walk with us daily on the community page. We read one chapter of the New Testament together every Monday through Friday. You'll have already met the Jesus you'll spend the rest of the year reading about.
